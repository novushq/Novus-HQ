Privacy Policy

Ring Sizer (app.novushq.ringsizer) — Developed by Novus HQ

Effective: November 26, 2026
Last updated: November 26, 2026
In Short (TL;DR)
Ring Sizer has no accounts and no developer server. Everything you enter — saved people, finger measurements, notes, screen calibration, and photos you capture or select for camera sizing — stays strictly on your Android device. The app displays ads via Google AdMob (with Google UMP consent management), which collects standard advertising and device diagnostics as described below. We never sell your personal data.

1. What Ring Sizer Is

Ring Sizer (app.novushq.ringsizer) is an Android utility application for calibrating your phone screen to physical millimetres, measuring ring and finger sizes using five methods, converting sizes across international ISO 8653 standards, generating printable PDF sizers, and keeping saved finger sizes for yourself and people you shop for.

The App is developed, owned, and operated by Novus HQ.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Data You Enter Inside the App (Stored Locally Only)

Ring Sizer stores the following information exclusively on your device inside its private Android Room database and DataStore preferences. None of this data is transmitted to Novus HQ or any external server:

  • Saved people profiles: names and relation labels (e.g. Me, Partner, Parent) that you add in Saved sizes.
  • Ring & finger measurements: inside diameter (mm), circumference (mm), US/ISO size, measurement method, hand and finger slot, band width, optional knuckle width, comfort-fit setting, calibration/confidence flags, timestamps, and optional text notes.
  • Screen calibration & app preferences: your calibrated mmPerPx scale factor, calibration method, coin verification result, preferred sizing system (US, UK, EU, JP, India, FR, Brazil), unit preference (mm or inch), theme choice, and local yearly reminder toggle.
  • Temporary share/camera files: temporary PNG size cards, generated printable PDF sizers, and camera-sizing photos stored in your app's private cache directory. Photos are processed locally on-device and are never uploaded. Share files leave your device only when you explicitly pick a destination in Android's share sheet.

2.2 Data Collected Automatically by Third-Party Advertising SDKs

Novus HQ does not run an analytics or user-tracking backend. However, Ring Sizer integrates the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK to display ads and collect regional privacy consent. Those Google SDKs may automatically collect:

Data Category Third-Party Provider Primary Purpose
Device or other IDs (Android Advertising ID / App Set ID) Google AdMob Serving personalised or non-personalised ads, frequency capping, and invalid-traffic fraud prevention
Approximate location (derived from IP address) Google AdMob & Google UMP Regional ad relevance and determining applicable privacy consent forms (EEA, UK, Switzerland, US states)
App interactions & SDK diagnostics (ad impressions, taps, SDK crash/latency metrics) Google AdMob Ad delivery measurement, performance reliability, and SDK stability
Consent choices (IABTCF_*, UMP_*) Google UMP (stored locally in SharedPreferences) Remembering whether you granted or declined personalised ad consent

2.3 What We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not require accounts, email addresses, phone numbers, or sign-in.
  • We do not upload your saved names, ring sizes, notes, or camera photos to any server.
  • We do not access precise GPS location, contacts, call logs, SMS, or microphone audio.

3. Device Permissions

Ring Sizer requests only permissions required for specific features. You can revoke optional permissions at any time in your Android system Settings.

  • Camera (android.permission.CAMERA — Prominent Disclosure): Requested only when you tap Take photo inside the Camera measurement method to photograph a ring beside a bank card or coin. The image is analyzed strictly on-device in memory and app cache to estimate inside diameter. Photos are never uploaded to Novus HQ or any third party. Camera access is optional — if denied, you can select an image through Android's system photo picker or align manually.
  • Notifications (android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Optional. Used only if you enable the local yearly re-measure check-in toggle in Saved sizes or Settings. Notifications are scheduled locally on your device via Android WorkManager and use generic wording that never mentions saved names or gift contexts.
  • Internet & Network State (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): Used to load Google AdMob ads, check connectivity for the ad banner / offline notice, and refresh Google UMP consent status. Core sizing features work offline.

4. How Data Is Used

  • Local profiles, measurements & calibration: Used exclusively to render true-scale gauges, compute ISO 8653 sizes and live fit advice, display your saved history, and export printable PDFs or share cards when you ask.
  • Advertising & consent signals (Google AdMob / UMP): Used by Google to serve teen-safe ads (MAX_AD_CONTENT_RATING_T), honour your consent preferences, and keep the app free without paywalls.

6. Advertising & Consent

Ring Sizer is free and ad-supported using Google AdMob:

  • Banner ads: Displayed cleanly above the bottom navigation bar on top-level tabs and never overlaid on active sizing circles, camera measurement, onboarding, or your Result screen.
  • Interstitial ads: Paced strictly at natural breaks when returning to a top-level tab after completed tasks — never blocking a sizing result.
  • Rewarded ads (opt-in): Available only if you tap Go ad-free for an hour in Settings to temporarily hide all ads. No sizing feature is locked behind watching an ad.
  • Consent & opting out of personalised ads: In regions requiring consent (such as the EEA, UK, Switzerland, and applicable US states), Google's UMP consent form lets you choose personalised or non-personalised ads. Declining personalised consent still lets you use the app normally with limited/non-personalised ads. You can change your choice anytime in Settings → Ad privacy choices (where applicable) or reset/delete your Android Advertising ID via Android Settings → Google → Ads (or Privacy → Ads).

7. Payments

Ring Sizer has no in-app purchases, paid tiers, or subscriptions. No payment or billing data is collected.

8. Data Sharing

Novus HQ never sells, trades, or rents your personal data. Your ring profiles, measurements, calibration data, and photos are never transmitted to Novus HQ or third parties (except when you voluntarily share a size card, text, or PDF via Android's share sheet). Only the third-party advertising SDKs listed in Section 5 receive standard device/ad telemetry to serve ads.

9. Your Rights

Depending on where you live (including the EU/EEA and UK under GDPR, or California and other US states under CCPA/CPRA), you have the right to access, correct, delete, or withdraw consent for your data:

  • Access, edit & delete local data: Edit or delete individual measurements (with undo) or delete any saved person directly inside the App, clear the App's storage in Android Settings, or uninstall the App to permanently remove all local data.
  • Withdraw ad consent: Update your consent anytime in Settings → Ad privacy choices (in applicable regions) or reset your Advertising ID in Android Settings.
  • Contact us: Email support@novushq.app with any privacy question or request.

10. Children's Privacy

Ring Sizer is a general-audience tool (configured with a teen-safe MAX_AD_CONTENT_RATING_T ad ceiling) and is not directed to children under the age of 13. While the sizing chart includes kids' ring sizes so parents or gift buyers can shop for children, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal data to us, contact support@novushq.app so we can assist immediately.

11. Security & Retention

Security: All user-entered data is stored locally inside Android's application sandbox. Any network traffic generated by the Google AdMob and UMP SDKs is encrypted in transit over HTTPS (cleartext HTTP traffic is disabled app-wide via usesCleartextTraffic="false").

Retention: Your saved profiles, measurements, and calibration settings remain on your device for as long as you keep them. Deleting a profile cascades and removes all associated measurements immediately; clearing app data or uninstalling removes everything from the device. Data handled by Google AdMob is retained according to Google's privacy policy.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Ring Sizer adds features or when platform requirements change. Updates are posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date at the top.
