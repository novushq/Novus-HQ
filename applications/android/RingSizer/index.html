About Ring Sizer

Most ring sizing apps render circles in abstract screen pixels and hide your size behind a subscription wall. Ring Sizer V2 starts with physical accuracy: calibrate your display once against a standard 85.60 × 53.98 mm bank card, a known coin, or a physical ruler, and optionally verify your calibration within ±0.3 mm with a coin in 10 seconds.

Measure five ways — lay an existing ring on the true-scale 120-tick gauge, measure finger base and knuckle width, wrap a paper strip, enter caliper/mandrel values directly, or photograph a ring beside a card or coin using on-device detection with full manual corner adjustment. Every result displays all seven international sizing systems (US/CA, UK/AU, EU/ISO, JP/CN/KR, India, FR/IT/ES, Brazil), an honest confidence score, and live Fit Advice for wide bands, comfort-fit interiors and prominent knuckles.

Keep sizes across all 10 fingers for yourself and family members privately on your phone, size surprise gifts quietly without giveaway alerts, share clean result cards, or print a verified ISO 8653 PDF sizer complete with a 30.0 mm ruler scale check.

Why use Ring Sizer?

Match a bank card, coin, or ruler once so every sizing circle and band-width preview renders in true physical millimetres, backed by an ISO 8653 lookup table with zero guesswork formulas.
