About SubTrack

SubTrack is designed to take the friction out of subscription management. With the rise of software as a service (SaaS), streaming subscriptions, and recurring utility bills, it is easy to lose track of where your hard-earned money goes. SubTrack gives you a comprehensive dashboard that gathers all your subscription commitments in one safe, local database on your Android device.

Key Highlights & Philosophy

SubTrack stores and processes your data on your device. We believe your financial logs should remain private to you. There are no remote tracking servers, no automated cloud scraping of your bank accounts, and no data selling. Your logs belong entirely inside your phone's sandbox.

Core Capabilities

  • Local Storage Sandbox: All inputted cost details, cycles, renewal dates, and notes are cached locally on device storage.
  • Dynamic Analytics & Cost Projections: View summaries of your active spending categorized by month, week, or year. Instant indicators will tell you how much your total recurring footprint grows.
  • Smart Push Notifications: Configure alert times to get notified 1, 2, or 5 days before a trial expires or a bill renews, avoiding accidental renewals.
  • Custom Currencies & Categories: Supports multiple currency metrics and custom labels to sort your services (e.g. Work, Entertainment, Utilities).

Why use SubTrack?

100% Offline & Private

All subscription data stays locally on your device. We do not run tracking servers or gather analytics on your finances.

Sleek Visual Reports

Understand where your money goes with elegant category breakdowns, usage insights, and interactive monthly analytics.

Smart Reminders

Never get caught off guard. Get timely local push notifications before your trials expire or subscription renews.
