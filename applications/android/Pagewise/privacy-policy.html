Pagewise Privacy Policy

Developed by Novus HQ · Package: app.novushhq.pagewise

Effective and updated: October 21, 2026

Summary

Pagewise is an offline PDF reader, editor and scanner. It does not collect, transmit, sell or share your personal data or documents with Novus HQ or anyone else. The app has no account, no advertising, no analytics or crash-reporting SDK, and no INTERNET permission, so it cannot send data over the network.

Documents and images you open

You choose every file Pagewise works with through Android’s document picker, photo picker, “Open with”/“Share” from another app, or the system camera app. Pagewise reads only the items you select. To process a file it may make a temporary working copy in the app’s private cache; you can remove these copies at any time with Settings → Clear temporary files, and Android may also clear the cache.

Files Pagewise creates

Results such as merged, split, compressed, watermarked, locked or converted PDFs, exported images and text files are saved on your device in Documents/Pagewise (on Android 10 and older, in Pagewise’s own app folder). They are not uploaded anywhere. You can open, share or delete them in the Files tab or with any file manager. Files in Documents/Pagewise remain on your device if you uninstall the app.

Camera

The scanner asks Android’s camera app to take a photo and hand it back to Pagewise. Pagewise does not hold the camera permission and cannot use the camera in the background. Photos are used only to build the PDF you save.

Passwords

Passwords you enter to lock, unlock or read a protected PDF are used only on your device for that operation. They are not stored or transmitted. Pagewise cannot recover a forgotten password.

Stored on your device

Pagewise keeps your settings (theme, default compression and similar preferences) and a list of files you imported or favorited, including their names and access links, in its private app storage. This list never leaves your device.

Sharing

When you tap Share or “Open with another app”, Android hands the file to the app you choose. That app’s privacy policy then applies to the copy it receives.

Android device backup

If Android backup is enabled, Android may include Pagewise’s settings and file list in your own device backup under your Google and device settings. Novus HQ cannot access that backup.

Deletion

Remove items from the Files list, delete saved files, clear temporary files in Settings, or clear app storage / uninstall Pagewise to remove its private data. We have no server copy to retrieve or erase.

Children

Pagewise is a general productivity tool and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Security

Android’s app sandbox protects Pagewise’s private storage. Locked PDFs use AES-256 encryption. Files you save to shared storage can be read by you and by apps you grant access to, so keep sensitive documents protected.

Contact and changes

If you email support, we receive only what you choose to send and use it to reply. Please don’t attach sensitive documents unless necessary. Changes to this policy will be published on this page with a new date.
