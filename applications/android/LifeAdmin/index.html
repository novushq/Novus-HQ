About Life Admin

Remember insurance renewals, passport expiry dates, warranties, free trials and household paperwork. Add a due date, choose when to be reminded, and see what needs attention in your upcoming list or monthly calendar.

Mark items renewed to keep history and move repeating reminders forward. Use custom lead times and month-based repeats, search notes, filter categories, or duplicate reminders to save setup time.

Life Admin is free and works offline without accounts, ads, analytics or in-app purchases. Export readable JSON backups to a location you choose and preview before replacing local data. Notification timing depends on Android battery settings.

Life Admin is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any medical condition. Medicine restock reminders are organizational aids. Consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.

Why use Life Admin?

Private by design

Reminders your way
