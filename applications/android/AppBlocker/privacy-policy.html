Privacy Policy

FocusGuard — App Blocker by NovusHQ

Effective: October 25, 2026
Updated: October 25, 2026
In Short (TL;DR)
FocusGuard is free and supported by ads from Google AdMob. Your block rules, schedules, keyword lists, settings and usage statistics stay in the App's private storage on your phone — they are never sent to us, to Google or to advertisers. We operate no servers and no account system. The only data that leaves your device is what Google's ads SDK needs to show an ad (device and ad identifiers, IP address, approximate location derived from it, and ad interaction data), governed by your consent choices and Google's privacy policy. Blocking works offline; ads need the internet.

1. What FocusGuard Is

FocusGuard (listing name FocusGuard: Block Reels & Apps, package app.novushq.appblocker) is an Android app blocker. It blocks distracting apps and websites on schedules you define, closes Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, TikTok and other short-form feeds the moment they open, runs focus timers and daily app limits, and shows on-device screen-time statistics and weekly reports.

FocusGuard is developed and operated by NovusHQ. Contact: support@novushq.app.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Data You Create (Stored on Your Device Only)

Everything the App knows about you is created by you, on your phone, and stays there. This includes:

  • Block rules: chosen apps and websites, schedules, days and start/end times.
  • Focus sessions, daily time limits, open limits, strict-mode state and pause delays.
  • Blocked keywords, adult-content filter setting and theme choice.
  • On-device usage statistics the App reads from Android (per-app screen time, session counts, hourly buckets) and the weekly reports it derives from them.

This data lives in the App's private local storage (Room database, DataStore and SharedPreferences) inside Android's app sandbox. We operate no servers and run no account system, and the App never sends this data anywhere — including to the ads SDK.

2.2 Data Collected Automatically: Advertising Only

FocusGuard contains no analytics SDKs, no crash-reporting SDKs and no account or login code. It does contain one third-party SDK: Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) together with Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) for consent. To serve, measure and protect ads, Google may collect:

  • Device identifiers, including the Android Advertising ID and app set ID.
  • IP address and the approximate (coarse) location derived from it.
  • Ad interactions (impressions, clicks, video completions) and app diagnostics such as crash data from the ads SDK.
  • Device information (model, OS version, language, network type).

NovusHQ does not receive this data in an identifiable form; we only see aggregate earnings reports in AdMob. The App never passes your block rules, the apps you block, your screen-time statistics or anything the accessibility service observes to the ads SDK.

The only email address involved is support@novushq.app, and only if you choose to write to us. Support emails are used solely to answer you.

2.3 Android Permissions — What They Do and Do Not Do

  • Accessibility service (required for blocking). The service observes which app or web page is in the foreground and whether a Reels/Shorts player is visible, so it can enforce your rules. It does not read message content, typing, passwords or page content beyond the address/domain being checked, and everything it sees stays in the phone's memory — it is never written to storage or transmitted.
  • Usage access (optional). Used only to compute the on-device statistics shown in Usage and Reports and to enforce daily time limits.
  • Notifications (optional, Android 13+). Used only to show the focus-session countdown.
  • Internet and network state. Used only by the ads SDK to load ads and consent messages. Blocking, schedules, limits and statistics work fully offline.
  • Advertising ID (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID, added by the ads SDK). Used by Google for ad delivery and measurement. You can reset or delete it in Android Settings > Privacy > Ads.

3. How Your Data Is Used

All processing happens on your device, to:

  • Enforce your block rules at the scheduled times (apps, websites, keywords, adult-content filter, Reels/Shorts detection).
  • Run focus sessions, daily time limits, open limits and strict mode.
  • Compute the Usage dashboard, per-app drill-downs and weekly Reports from Android usage statistics.
  • Show the focus-session countdown notification, if you enabled notifications.

Separately, the ads SDK uses the data listed in 2.2 to show ads in the App (a banner, an occasional full-screen ad between tasks, and an optional video you can watch to go ad-free for an hour). NovusHQ runs no analytics pipeline and no server-side processing of any kind.

4. Third-Party Services

FocusGuard embeds exactly one third-party service:

  • Google AdMob and User Messaging Platform — ads and consent. Google Privacy Policy · How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

There are no analytics, crash-reporting, attribution or payment SDKs.

When you install or update the App through Google Play, Google's own store processes apply (account, download delivery); those are governed by Google's privacy policy and are outside this App's control.

5. Advertising

FocusGuard is free for everyone and paid for by ads. We keep them out of the way: one banner at the bottom of the screen (never during onboarding or permission setup), an occasional full-screen ad only when you finish a task and return to a tab (at most once every few minutes, never on launch), and an optional video you choose to watch in Settings to hide all ads for an hour. The blocking screens the App shows over other apps never contain ads. Ad content is limited to teen-appropriate categories.

Consent. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, and in US states that require it, Google's consent message appears when you first open the App. Declining is respected: ads become non-personalised (limited ads) and the App keeps working fully. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Ads > Ad privacy choices. Everywhere, you can opt out of personalised ads by deleting or resetting your Advertising ID in Android Settings > Privacy > Ads.

6. Payments

FocusGuard currently processes no payments: there are no in-app purchases or subscriptions in this version, and we never see or store payment details. If a Pro upgrade via Google Play billing is introduced later, this policy will be updated before it ships, and payments will be processed solely by Google Play.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade or rent any data, and we hold none about you on any server. Your rules and statistics never leave your device. The ads SDK shares the data described in section 2.2 with Google and, where you have consented, Google's ad partners, to show and measure ads; under some US state laws this may count as "sharing" for cross-context advertising, which you can decline through the consent message or Ad privacy choices. Emails you send to support@novushq.app are used only to answer you. We may preserve or disclose information if required by law.

8. Your Rights

Because all of your data stays on your device, you are always in control: view or change your rules and settings in the App at any time, clear the App's storage (Android Settings > Apps > FocusGuard > Storage > Clear data) to erase everything, or uninstall the App — uninstalling removes all local data.

8A. GDPR Rights (EEA / UK / Switzerland)

If you are in the European Economic Area, UK, or Switzerland, you have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection. We hold no data about you on any server; for advertising data processed by Google, use Ad privacy choices in the App or Google's tools at myadcenter.google.com. For anything else, write to support@novushq.app with any question and we respond within 30 days.

8B. CCPA / CPRA Rights (California)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to know, delete and correct personal information and to opt out of sales. We do not sell personal information. You can opt out of sharing for personalised ads via the consent message, Settings > Ads > Ad privacy choices, or by deleting your Advertising ID. To exercise other rights, email support@novushq.app. We respond within 45 days.

8C. Other Regions

We comply with applicable privacy laws in all regions where FocusGuard is available, including Brazil (LGPD), India (DPDP), Canada (PIPEDA), Australia, South Korea, and Japan. Contact us to exercise your rights.

9. Children's Privacy

FocusGuard is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. Ads are restricted to teen-appropriate content. If you believe a child has sent us an email with personal data, contact us and we will delete it immediately.

10. Security & Retention

10.1 Data Security

The App itself sends none of your rules or statistics anywhere; ad traffic is encrypted in transit by Google's SDK (HTTPS). On your phone, your data is protected by Android's application sandbox — other apps cannot read it — and your rules and statistics are only ever processed in memory or in the App's private storage.

10.2 Data Retention

  • Your rules, settings and statistics — retained on your device until you clear the App's data or uninstall the App.
  • Accessibility observations — never retained; discarded immediately after each block decision.
  • Advertising data — retained by Google according to its own retention policy; NovusHQ does not store it.
  • Support emails you send us — kept only as long as needed to resolve your request.
