Privacy Policy

Codeforcesly by Novus HQ

Effective: October 26, 2026

Codeforcesly (“the app”) is published by Novus HQ. This policy explains what information the app accesses, what is collected by the advertising services it uses, where data is kept, and how to contact us. Starting with version 2.0 the app is free and supported by ads. Earlier versions contained no advertising.

1. Information the app accesses

When you enter a Codeforces handle, Codeforcesly requests publicly available profile, rating, contest, submission, problem, blog and leaderboard information from the public Codeforces API and loads profile pictures from Codeforces servers. This is public data, not private account data. The app never asks for your Codeforces password and has no account system of its own.

Your handle, the friends you choose to save, your theme choice and cached Codeforces responses are stored only on your device. Novus HQ does not receive them.

2. Advertising data collected by Google

Codeforcesly shows an adaptive banner ad, occasional full-screen ads at natural breaks (for example when you return from a contest or problem screen), and an optional rewarded video in Settings that hides all ads for one hour. Ads are provided by Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK. No ads are shown during sign-in, while reading a problem statement or while reading a blog post.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share with Google: your IP address (which can be used to estimate approximate location), device and advertising identifiers such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID, ad interactions such as views and taps, and diagnostic information about ad performance. Google uses this information to serve and measure ads, for analytics and to prevent fraud. This data is encrypted in transit. Your Codeforces handle, saved friends and app content are never sent to AdMob. Ads are requested with a maximum content rating of Teen.

Learn more in Google’s Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

3. Consent choices

Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland) the app asks for your consent through Google’s User Messaging Platform before any ad is requested, and offers “Ad privacy choices” in Settings so you can change your decision at any time. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings and manage ad preferences in My Ad Center. Declining personalised ads does not limit any app feature. The app stores your consent status and any earned ad-free period on your device.

4. Sharing and third parties

Novus HQ does not sell your information and does not run its own servers for this app. Data leaves your device only to request public data from Codeforces and to request ads from Google as described above. Google Play may process standard app-store information under its own policies. Codeforcesly is not affiliated with or endorsed by Codeforces.

5. Storage and deletion

Use Settings → Sign out to remove your handle and saved friends, and Settings → Clear cached data to remove cached Codeforces responses. Clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling Codeforcesly removes all locally stored data. Google determines how long it retains advertising data; use the Google controls linked above. To ask about information you sent us by email, contact support.

6. Children’s privacy

Codeforcesly is a tool for competitive programmers and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact support so we can address it.

7. Changes to this policy

We update this policy when the app’s behaviour changes. The effective date above changes with each revision.

8. Contact

Questions, privacy requests or app feedback: support@novushq.app.
