Privacy Policy

Shaadi Biodata Maker — Developed by Novus HQ

Effective: November 15, 2026
Last updated: November 21, 2026
Privacy summary
Shaadi Biodata Maker stores profiles and selected photos on your device without an account. You choose where to export, share or back up copies. Google AdMob may collect device identifiers and other information to serve and measure ads.

1. What Shaadi Biodata Maker Is

Shaadi Biodata Maker is a mobile application that helps you create a marriage biodata from guided fields and templates, preview it, and export it as a PDF or image to share.

The App is developed, owned, and operated by Novus HQ.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Biodata and preferences

The app stores what you enter, such as names, birth details, education, work, family information, contact details, and any religious or horoscope information you choose to include. It also saves up to three photos per profile, layout choices and app preferences. Novus HQ receives your information when you send it to us, such as in a support email. The editor has no account or server sync.

2.2 Data collected automatically

Google AdMob may collect the advertising ID or other device identifiers, IP address, approximate location derived from IP, app and device information, interactions with ads, and diagnostics. Google User Messaging Platform handles ad consent choices. On supported Android versions, the ad SDK may use Android Privacy Sandbox features to select or measure ads. The app's own diagnostic events are written to Android's local log.

2.3 Android permissions

The app uses internet and network status for ads, consent and purchases when available. It uses the system photo picker to read images you select. On Android 8 and 9, the app requests storage permission when needed to save exports to Downloads. You can deny or revoke it in Android's app settings. Android 10 and later use Android's shared media storage without that permission. The app requests no direct access to precise location, contacts, microphone or camera.

3. How Your Data Is Used

  • Profile details and photos are used to edit, preview, print, export and restore biodatas.
  • Local preferences remember your settings and manage how often the app shows ads.
  • Google uses advertising data to serve and measure ads and help prevent fraud. Consent choices determine which ads can be requested.
  • Purchase data is used to verify and restore Pro access if paid purchases are offered.
  • We use the information you send to respond to your support or privacy request.

5. Advertising

The free version can show banner, native, full-screen and rewarded ads through Google AdMob. Google's consent form is shown where required. Depending on your consent and Google's settings, ads may be personalised or non-personalised. When the option is available, go to Settings → Ads → Ad privacy choices to change or withdraw consent. You can reset or delete the advertising ID in Android's ad settings. The location of that setting varies by Android version.

6. Payments

If paid Pro access is offered, Google Play processes the payment and RevenueCat may verify your purchase or restore access. Novus HQ and the app do not receive payment card details.

7. Data Sharing

Novus HQ does not sell your personal data. The app does not send profile details or selected photos to advertising or purchase services. If you share or print an export, the app or recipient you choose gets a copy. If you save a ZIP backup through a cloud file provider, that provider receives the backup. Android can also back up eligible app data if device backup is enabled. Those services handle their copies under their own terms.

8. Your Rights

You can view, correct, export and delete profiles in the app. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights to access, correct, obtain a copy of or request deletion of personal data, or to object to certain uses. For information held by Novus HQ, email support@novushq.app. You can manage ad consent as described in Advertising.

9. Children's Privacy

The app is intended for adults preparing marriage profiles. It is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has sent personal information to us, contact us so we can address it.

10. Security, Retention and Deletion

10.1 Security

Android's app sandbox protects the profile database and photos kept in private app storage. Google and RevenueCat use encrypted connections for their services. Exported documents and ZIP backups are readable files. Store them in a location you trust and share them with intended recipients.

10.2 Retention and deletion

Profiles and photos are kept until you delete them in My Biodatas, clear the app's storage in Android Settings or uninstall the app. Clearing storage or uninstalling removes the app's local preferences too. Separately delete copies in Downloads, backups and shared destinations. You can manage device backups in Android settings. Google and RevenueCat retain data under their own privacy policies. We keep support correspondence as long as needed to respond and meet legal obligations.
