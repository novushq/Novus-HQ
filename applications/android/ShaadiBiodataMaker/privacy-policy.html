Privacy Policy

Shaadi Biodata Maker — Developed by Novus HQ

Effective: November 15, 2026
Updated: November 15, 2026
In Short (TL;DR)
Your biodata and photos are stored only on your phone. We have no accounts and our servers never receive your details. The free version shows ads through Google AdMob, which may use your device advertising ID. You choose when to export or share a file.

1. What Shaadi Biodata Maker Is

Shaadi Biodata Maker is a mobile application that helps you create a marriage biodata from guided fields and templates, preview it, and export it as a PDF or image to share.

The App is developed, owned, and operated by Novus HQ.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Data You Provide Voluntarily

We collect and store data that you explicitly enter inside the App. Depending on your configuration, this may remain stored purely locally on your device or be synchronized with a remote server:

  • Biodata details you type in (such as name, date of birth, family, education and contact details), up to three photos you choose, and your app settings. This information is stored only on your device. It is not uploaded to us.

2.2 Data Collected Automatically by Third Parties / Services

To show ads in the free version, the advertising provider below may collect the following information from your device:

Data Category Third-Party Provider Primary Purpose
Advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, device and ad interaction dataGoogle AdMob (Google LLC)Showing and measuring ads, limiting ad frequency, fraud prevention, and, where you consent, personalised ads

2.3 What We Do NOT Collect

We prioritize your privacy and do not request access to unrelated device systems. We do not access or collect:

  • Your precise location, contacts, microphone or camera.
  • Your biodata text or photos. They stay on your device; we never receive them.
  • Payment card details. Any purchase is handled by Google Play.

3. How Your Data Is Used

We use the collected information for the following specific purposes:

  • To provide and maintain the core functionalities of the App.
  • To remember your settings on your device.
  • To show ads that keep the free version available, and to limit how often they appear.

5. Advertising & Analytics

The free version shows banner ads, occasional full-screen ads and optional rewarded videos through Google AdMob. In regions where it is required, you are asked for consent before personalised ads, and you can change your choice later in the App settings. The App does not use any other analytics service. You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time:

  • Android: Settings > Google > Ads > Reset/Delete Advertising ID.

6. Payments

All features are currently free to use. If a paid Pro option is offered, the purchase is processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your card details.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal data. Sharing only occurs with the trusted third-party providers mentioned in Section 4 to facilitate app operations, or if mandated by law enforcement.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your geographic location (e.g. EU/EEA under GDPR, California under CCPA/CPRA, etc.), you have specific statutory rights concerning your personal information, including the right to access, rectify, port, or request erasure of your data.

To exercise any of your rights, please reach out to us at support@novushq.app.

9. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly target or collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you become aware that a child has provided us with personal data, please notify us immediately so we can remove it.

10. Security & Retention

10.1 Data Security

Your biodata stays in the App's private storage on your device and is protected by Android's app sandbox. Traffic to the ad provider uses TLS. When you export or share a file, you choose the app that receives it, so only share with people you trust.

10.2 Data Retention

We hold no copy of your biodata. It is kept on your device until you delete it or uninstall the App, which removes it. Backups you create with the in-app backup feature are files you save and control. If Android device backup is turned on, Google may back up app data under its own terms.
