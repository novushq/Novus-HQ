About Shaadi Biodata Maker

Fill in personal, horoscope, education, family and contact details and add up to three photos. Keep separate profiles for family members. Change templates any time and keep the details you already entered.

Choose labels in English, Hindi, Marathi, Gujarati, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam, Punjabi, Urdu, Odia, Assamese, Nepali, Konkani or Sindhi. Preview your layout before you save, print or share it.

Why use Shaadi Biodata Maker?

90 designs, 16 languages

Browse 15 collections, from regional and religious designs to floral, royal and minimal layouts. Urdu labels read right to left.

Make it yours

Choose from 24 page patterns and customize header symbols, colours, fonts and borders. Reorder, hide or add fields and sections.

Profiles stay on your device

Create profiles without an account. Export or share copies when you choose, and back up your profiles to a ZIP file.
