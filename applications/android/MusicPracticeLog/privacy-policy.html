Privacy Policy

Music Practice Log — Developed by Novus HQ

Effective: November 23, 2026
Last updated: November 23, 2026
In Short (TL;DR)
Your practice journal (sessions, notes, instruments, goals and settings) is stored only on your phone. There is no account, no cloud sync and no backend server of ours, and Novus HQ never receives your practice data. The App shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects device and advertising data (with consent where the law requires it), and uses Google Play and RevenueCat to sell and verify the optional one-time Pro upgrade. We never sell your data.

1. What Music Practice Log Is

Music Practice Log (the “App”, package app.novushq.musicpracticelog) is a mobile application that lets musicians time or log their practice sessions, write notes about what they practised, set daily and weekly practice goals, and see streaks and statistics, all without creating an account.

The App is developed, owned, and operated by Novus HQ (“we”, “us”). This policy covers only the App.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Data You Enter (stays on your device)

Everything you type or choose in the App is saved in a private database and settings file inside the App's own storage on your phone. It is not uploaded to Novus HQ or to any third party, and it is excluded from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer.

  • Practice sessions: the practice date, duration (in seconds), the instrument or equipment name, the optional focus you type (for example a piece or technique), optional notes, whether the session was timed or added manually, and when it was created and last edited.
  • Active timer: while a stopwatch is running or paused, an unsaved draft with the same fields plus elapsed time, start time, time zone and a device-boot marker, so the timer survives the App being closed or the phone restarting.
  • Goals: your optional daily minutes target and weekly practice-days target.
  • Instruments and preferences: your default instrument, the custom instruments or gear you add, theme (light/dark/system), dynamic-colour choice and whether you finished the first-use setup.

2.2 Data Stored or Collected Automatically

The App itself has no analytics, crash reporting or telemetry. It keeps a few technical values on your device only: when Pro ownership was last verified, the number of reward videos watched and the end of any ad-free window you earned, and a count of launches made without an internet connection. The third-party services built into the App collect the following:

2.3 Data Accessed Through Permissions

Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS). On Android 13 and later the App asks for this permission the first time a practice timer is running. It is used only to show the ongoing stopwatch notification with Pause, Resume and Finish buttons while a timer is active. The notification shows your instrument and elapsed time and is created on your device; nothing about it is sent anywhere. If you decline, the timer still works inside the App. You can turn notifications on or off at any time in Android Settings > Apps > Music Practice Log > Notifications.

The App also declares these permissions, which Android grants automatically and which give no access to personal data:

  • Internet (INTERNET) and network state (ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): used to load ads, show the consent form, and buy or restore Pro, and to check whether you are online so these are retried when the connection returns. Your practice data is never sent over the network.
  • Foreground service (FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USE): keeps the stopwatch you started running in the background until you pause or finish it.

2.4 What We Do NOT Collect

The App does not request or access your location (GPS), camera, microphone, contacts, photos, media files, SMS, call logs or the list of other apps. It does not record audio. We do not see your payment card details. There is no sign-in, so we do not hold your name, email address or any profile.

3. How Your Data Is Used

  • Practice sessions, timer draft, goals and instruments: to show your log, today's progress, streaks and statistics on your device, and to build an export file when you ask for one.
  • Preferences: to apply your theme and defaults.
  • Notification permission: to show the running timer's controls.
  • Advertising ID, device information and ad interactions (Google AdMob): to serve and measure ads, which keep the core App free.
  • Consent choices (Google UMP): to respect your choice about how ads may use your data.
  • Anonymous app user ID and purchase history (RevenueCat, Google Play): to unlock and restore the Pro upgrade.
  • Reward-video count and ad-free window (on device): to hide ads for 40 minutes after you choose to watch two reward videos.

We do not use your data for profiling by Novus HQ, and we never use your practice records for advertising.

5. Advertising

The App is supported by ads served by Google AdMob: a banner on some screens, an occasional full-screen ad at a natural break after you finish something, and optional reward videos you can choose to watch in Settings to hide all ads for 40 minutes. Ads are limited to content rated for teens or milder. Owners of Pro see no ads.

  • Consent: before any ad is requested, the App checks your consent status with Google's User Messaging Platform. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland) a consent form is shown, and no ads are requested until you have answered. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Ad privacy choices.
  • Personalised and non-personalised ads: if you consent, Google may use your advertising ID and device data to show ads based on your interests. If you do not, Google shows non-personalised ads, which still use limited data (such as approximate location from your IP address) for delivery, frequency capping, measurement and fraud prevention.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in Android Settings > Google > Ads (on newer phones: Settings > Security & privacy > Privacy > Ads). Deleting it stops it being used for personalised ads.

The App contains no analytics SDK. Your practice records are never shared with advertisers.

6. Payments

Logging, the timer, goals, streaks and your full history are free. Music Practice Log Pro is an optional one-time purchase that unlocks detailed statistics and spreadsheet export and removes ads. During the current launch offer, the Pro statistics and export features are available to everyone at no cost (ads are still shown unless you own Pro).

Payments are processed entirely by Google Play under your Google account. Novus HQ never sees or stores your card or payment details. The App uses RevenueCat to confirm the purchase with Google Play: when the App starts and when you buy or restore Pro, RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase record for this App. The App then remembers on your device that Pro was verified, so Pro keeps working offline.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal data, and Novus HQ does not receive your practice data at all. Data leaves your device only in these cases:

  • Google AdMob and the Google User Messaging Platform receive the advertising and consent data described in Section 2.2, to show ads.
  • RevenueCat and Google Play receive purchase data, to sell and verify Pro.
  • Exports you create: when you export your journal (Excel workbook or CSV), the App writes the file only to the location you pick in Android's file picker. What happens to that file afterwards (for example if you save it to a cloud drive or share it) is up to you and that service.
  • If required by law, we would disclose the limited information we hold, which in practice is only the content of emails you send us.

8. Your Rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EU/EEA and UK under the GDPR, or California under the CCPA/CPRA), you have the right to access, correct, delete and export your personal data, to object to or restrict its use, to withdraw consent, and not to be discriminated against for using these rights.

  • Access, correct, export: your practice data is on your device; you can view and edit every session in the App and export it at any time.
  • Delete: see Section 10.3.
  • Withdraw consent: change ad consent in Settings > Ad privacy choices, reset your advertising ID in Android settings, or revoke the notification permission in Android settings.
  • Data held by third parties: requests about ad or purchase data can be made to Google and RevenueCat using the links in Section 4; we will help you if you contact us.

To exercise any of your rights, or if you have a complaint, email us at support@novushq.app. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

9. Children's Privacy

The App is a general-audience practice journal for independent learners, hobbyists and students. It is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal data through the App, email us at support@novushq.app and we will help remove it, including asking our ad and payment providers to delete what they hold.

10. Security, Retention & Deletion

10.1 Data Security

Your journal is stored in the App's private storage, which Android's app sandbox keeps unreadable to other apps. It is excluded from cloud backup and device transfer, so it never leaves the phone unless you export it. The ad and purchase SDKs communicate with their providers over encrypted HTTPS connections; the App does not allow unencrypted network traffic. No storage or transmission method is 100% secure, so please keep your phone locked.

10.2 Data Retention

  • Practice sessions, goals, instruments and preferences: kept on your device until you delete them, clear the App's data or uninstall the App.
  • Timer draft: kept only until you finish or discard the session.
  • Ad, consent and purchase data held by Google and RevenueCat: kept according to their own policies linked in Section 4. Your Pro purchase record stays with Google Play so you can restore it.
  • Emails you send us: kept only as long as needed to answer you.

10.3 How to Delete Your Data

  • In the App: delete single sessions from the log, or use Settings > Delete all practice data to permanently erase all sessions, the active timer, goals and the default instrument.
  • Clear app data or uninstall: Android Settings > Apps > Music Practice Log > Storage > Clear storage, or uninstalling, erases everything the App stored on the phone. Because there is no cloud backup, this cannot be undone.
  • Ad data: reset or delete your advertising ID as described in Section 5.
  • By email: we hold no account or practice data for you, but you can email support@novushq.app and we will help you request deletion of purchase or ad data from RevenueCat and Google.

11. Changes to This Policy

If the App starts handling data differently, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date above before the change ships, and describe significant changes in the App's release notes on Google Play. Where the law requires your consent to a change, we will ask for it in the App.
