Privacy Policy

Music Practice Log — Developed by Novus HQ

Effective: November 23, 2026
Last updated: November 24, 2026
In Short (TL;DR)
Your practice journal (sessions, notes, audio takes, routines, imported PDF scores, annotations, tempo presets, reminders, goals and settings) is stored only on your phone. There is no account, no cloud sync and no backend server of ours, and Novus HQ never receives your practice data. Microphone permission is requested strictly on demand when you open the Chromatic Tuner (in-memory pitch detection only) or record a local Audio Take. The App shows ads from Google AdMob (with consent where the law requires it) and uses Google Play Billing and RevenueCat for the optional one-time lifetime Pro upgrade, which removes ads. Exports (Excel, CSV, printable PDF reports and .mplbackup archives) are created on your phone and saved only where you choose. We never sell your data.

1. What Music Practice Log Is

Music Practice Log (the “App”, package app.novushq.musicpracticelog) is an offline musician toolkit and mobile application that lets musicians time or log practice sessions, practise with a built-in metronome, chromatic tuner and 12-note drone, record local audio takes, follow step-by-step practice routines, read and annotate local PDF sheet music, set goals and local reminders, and export or back up their data — all without creating an account.

The App is developed, owned, and operated by Novus HQ (“we”, “us”). This policy covers only the App.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Data You Enter (stays on your device)

Everything you type or choose in the App is saved in a private database and settings file inside the App's own storage on your phone. It is not uploaded to Novus HQ or to any third party, and it is excluded from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer.

  • Practice sessions & audio takes: the practice date, duration (in seconds), instrument or equipment name, optional focus, notes, target BPM, linked routine or PDF score, and any local AAC/M4A audio takes you choose to record in the App's private storage.
  • Active timer: while a stopwatch is running or paused, an unsaved draft with the same fields plus elapsed time, start time, time zone and a device-boot marker, so the timer survives the App being closed or the phone restarting.
  • Routines, PDF scores & tempo presets: reusable practice routines, saved metronome presets, local PDF sheet music files you import via Android's document picker or Share sheet, and any vector pen/highlighter annotations you draw on them.
  • Goals, reminders & preferences: your optional daily minutes target, weekly practice-days target, local practice reminder schedule, default and custom instruments, tuner/metronome preferences, and theme.
  • Exports & local backups you create: when you export, the App builds the file on your phone: an Excel workbook (.xlsx), a CSV file, a printable A4 PDF practice report, or a restorable .mplbackup archive. The App writes it only to the location you pick in Android's file picker and never uploads it.

The App itself has no analytics, crash reporting or telemetry. It keeps a few technical values on your device only: when Pro ownership was last verified, the number of reward videos watched, and the end of any ad-free window you earned. 2.3 Data Accessed Through Permissions

Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO). Requested strictly on demand when you explicitly tap the Chromatic Tuner or start recording an Audio Take — never on app launch. In the Chromatic Tuner, microphone audio is analysed strictly in memory on your device to measure pitch and is never saved or transmitted. When you record an Audio Take, the recording is saved only inside the App's private storage on your phone and is never uploaded anywhere unless you explicitly tap Share. You can use the Metronome, Drone Generator, timer, journal, routines, and PDF viewer without granting microphone access.

Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS). On Android 13 and later the App asks for this permission only when a practice timer starts or when you turn on optional local practice reminders. It is used to show the ongoing stopwatch notification (with Pause, Resume, Finish, and Stop click controls) and local WorkManager practice reminders. Nothing about your notifications is sent over the network. You can revoke permission at any time in Android Settings > Apps > Music Practice Log > Notifications.

The App also declares these permissions, which Android grants automatically and which give no access to personal data:

  • Internet (INTERNET) and network state (ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): used to load ads, show the consent form, and buy or restore Pro, and to check whether you are online so these are retried when the connection returns. Your practice data, recordings and PDFs are never sent over the network.
  • Foreground service (FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MICROPHONE): keeps your active practice stopwatch (and optional in-session audio recording) running accurately in the background while a session is active, and stops cleanly when you finish or discard it.

2.4 What We Do NOT Collect

The App does not request or access your location (GPS), camera, contacts, photos, SMS, call logs or the list of other apps. We do not upload your audio takes, PDFs, or journal entries. We do not see your payment card details. There is no sign-in, so we do not hold your name, email address or any profile.

3. How Your Data Is Used

  • Practice sessions, timer draft, routines, tempo presets, goals and instruments: to show your log, search results, today's progress, streaks, 12-week consistency heatmap, home screen widgets and statistics on your device, and to build export or backup files when you ask for them.
  • Microphone permission (on-demand only): to measure pitch strictly in memory when you open the Chromatic Tuner, or to record a local Audio Take inside the App's private storage when you tap Record.
  • Imported PDF scores & annotations: to render your sheet music offline and save your pen/highlighter strokes locally on your phone.
  • Preferences & local reminders: to apply your theme, tuner/metronome defaults, and schedule local WorkManager practice reminders with smart-skip.
  • Notification permission: to show the running timer's controls (Pause, Resume, Finish, Stop click) and deliver optional local practice reminders.
  • Advertising ID, device information and ad interactions (Google AdMob): to serve and measure ads, which keep the core App free.
  • Consent choices (Google UMP): to respect your choice about how ads may use your data.
  • Anonymous app user ID and purchase history (RevenueCat, Google Play): to unlock and restore the Pro upgrade.
  • Reward-video count and ad-free window (on device): to hide ads for 40 minutes after you choose to watch two reward videos.

We do not use your data for profiling by Novus HQ, and we never use your practice records, audio recordings or PDFs for advertising.

5. Advertising

The free version of the App is supported by ads served by Google AdMob. Ads are limited to content rated for teens or milder. Owners of Pro see no ads of any kind.

  • Adaptive banner: one anchored adaptive banner at the bottom of the screen, sized to your screen width.
  • Paced full-screen (interstitial) ads: shown only at a natural break, when you return to a main tab after completing a task (such as saving a session, saving your goals or finishing an export), and only after at least two completed tasks, never in the first minute of use and at most once every three minutes. Cancelling or backing out without finishing never triggers one.
  • Opt-in rewarded ads: in Settings you can choose to watch short reward videos; every two videos hide banners and full-screen ads for 40 minutes (stacking, up to 24 hours). The count of videos watched and the end time of your ad-free window are stored only on your device.
  • Consent (GDPR): on every launch, before any ad is requested, the App checks your consent status with Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland a consent form is shown, and no ads are requested until you have answered. If you withdraw consent, ad requests stop.
  • US state privacy laws: where a US state privacy law applies (such as California's CCPA/CPRA and other state laws that give you a right to opt out of targeted advertising or the “sale” or “sharing” of personal information), the same Google UMP tool lets you opt out of your data being used for personalised ads.
  • Changing your choice: wherever one of these laws applies to you, the App shows Settings > Ad privacy choices, which reopens Google's privacy form so you can change or withdraw your choice at any time.
  • Personalised and non-personalised ads: if you consent, Google may use your advertising ID and device data to show ads based on your interests. If you do not, Google shows non-personalised ads, which still use limited data (such as approximate location from your IP address) for delivery, frequency capping, measurement and fraud prevention.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in Android Settings > Google > Ads (on newer phones: Settings > Security & privacy > Privacy > Ads). Deleting it stops it being used for personalised ads.

The App contains no analytics SDK. Your practice records are never shared with advertisers.

6. Payments

Logging, the crash-safe timer, built-in metronome, chromatic tuner, 12-note drone, in-session audio recording, practice routines, offline PDF score viewer, instant search, 12-week heatmap, local reminders, home screen widgets, goals, streaks and your full history are free. Music Practice Log Pro is an optional one-time lifetime purchase (not a subscription) that unlocks detailed statistics & By Piece breakdowns, Audio A–B Loop & pitch-preserving slow-down (0.5×–1.5×), PDF score pen/highlighter annotation, Excel/CSV/PDF teacher reports, restorable .mplbackup local backups, and removes all ads forever. During the current launch offer, Pro features are available to everyone at no cost (ads are still shown unless you own Pro).

Payments are processed entirely by Google Play through Google Play Billing under your Google account. Novus HQ never sees or stores your card or payment details. The App uses RevenueCat to confirm the purchase with Google Play: when the App starts and when you buy or restore Pro, RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase record for this App. The App then remembers on your device that Pro was verified, so Pro keeps working offline.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal data, and Novus HQ does not receive your practice data, recordings or PDFs at all. Data leaves your device only in these cases:

  • Google AdMob and the Google User Messaging Platform receive the advertising and consent data described in Section 2.2, to show ads.
  • RevenueCat and Google Play receive purchase data, to sell and verify Pro.
  • Exports, backups & shared takes you initiate: when you export an Excel .xlsx workbook, CSV file, printable PDF report, or .mplbackup archive, or explicitly tap Share on an Audio Take, Android writes or hands that file only to the destination app or folder you choose; Novus HQ and the ad and payment providers never receive it.
  • If required by law, we would disclose the limited information we hold, which in practice is only the content of emails you send us.

8. Your Rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EU/EEA and UK under the GDPR, or California under the CCPA/CPRA), you have the right to access, correct, delete and export your personal data, to object to or restrict its use, to withdraw consent, and not to be discriminated against for using these rights.

  • Access, correct, export: your practice data is on your device; you can view and edit every session in the App and export or back it up at any time.
  • Delete: see Section 10.3.
  • Withdraw consent: change ad consent in Settings > Ad privacy choices, reset your advertising ID in Android settings, or revoke Microphone or Notification permissions at any time in Android Settings > Apps > Music Practice Log > Permissions.
  • Data held by third parties: requests about ad or purchase data can be made to Google and RevenueCat using the links in Section 4; we will help you if you contact us.

To exercise any of your rights, or if you have a complaint, email us at support@novushq.app. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

US state privacy rights: if you live in a US state with a consumer privacy law (such as California under the CCPA/CPRA), you may opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of your personal information for targeted advertising. Novus HQ does not sell your data for money; the only such use is Google's use of advertising data for personalised ads, which you can opt out of in Settings > Ad privacy choices (shown where your state's law applies) or by deleting your advertising ID. Buying Pro also removes all ads.

9. Children's Privacy

The App is a general-audience practice journal for independent learners, hobbyists and students. It is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal data through the App, email us at support@novushq.app and we will help remove it, including asking our ad and payment providers to delete what they hold.

10. Security, Retention & Deletion

10.1 Data Security

Your journal is stored in the App's private storage, which Android's app sandbox keeps unreadable to other apps. It is excluded from cloud backup and device transfer, so it never leaves the phone unless you export it. The ad and purchase SDKs communicate with their providers over encrypted HTTPS connections; the App does not allow unencrypted network traffic. No storage or transmission method is 100% secure, so please keep your phone locked.

10.2 Data Retention

  • Practice sessions, audio takes, routines, imported PDF scores, annotations, tempo presets, reminders, goals, instruments and preferences: kept in the App's private storage on your device until you delete them, clear the App's data or uninstall the App.
  • Timer draft: kept only until you finish or discard the session.
  • Ad, consent and purchase data held by Google and RevenueCat: kept according to their own policies linked in Section 4. Your Pro purchase record stays with Google Play so you can restore it.
  • Emails you send us: kept only as long as needed to answer you.

10.3 How to Delete Your Data

  • In the App: delete single sessions, audio takes, routines, or PDF scores individually, or use Settings > Delete all practice data to permanently erase all sessions, active timer, audio takes, routines, imported PDFs, tempo presets, reminders, goals and your default instrument.
  • Clear app data or uninstall: Android Settings > Apps > Music Practice Log > Storage > Clear storage, or uninstalling, erases everything the App stored on the phone. Because there is no cloud backup, this cannot be undone.
  • Ad data: reset or delete your advertising ID as described in Section 5.
  • By email: we hold no account or practice data for you, but you can email support@novushq.app and we will help you request deletion of purchase or ad data from RevenueCat and Google.
