About

Small, dependable apps.

Novus HQ builds Android apps that each do one job well. Every app is designed for phones and tablets, supports light and dark themes, and is maintained after release.

Focused scope

Each app is built around one clear task, and new features are added only when they make that task easier.

Clear about data

Every app has a plain-language privacy policy that states what is stored, where it is stored and what is shared.

Built for Android

Material design, adaptive layouts for tablets, and regular updates through Google Play.
